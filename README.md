# 🌲 Arboris: Gamified Local Tourism & EdTech

> 🌐 **Live Interactive App:** [Explore Arboris](https://arboris-monte-di-malo.ai.studio)

**Arboris** is a concept application aimed at youth and families passionate about mountains and learning[cite: 4]. The primary goal is to guide visitors through Monte di Malo by creating "controlled" foot traffic, allowing them to discover the territory's beauty through personal challenges and community engagement[cite: 4].

## 🎯 Core Concept & Features
*   **Interactive App:** Provides users with specific routes, trivia, and a tracking system for locked objectives[cite: 4].
*   **Gamification Elements:** Includes treasure hunts, events, and community challenges with prizes[cite: 4].
*   **Physical Integration:** Users scan QR codes at specific Totem Check Points to unlock deep-dive content about the surrounding area[cite: 4].
*   **Market Positioning:** Operates in a "Blue Ocean" space focused on local niche tourism, sitting between generic botanical identification apps and standardized indoor museum guides[cite: 4].

## 📍 Key Checkpoints
*   **Museo del Priaboniano "Renato Gasparella":** Explores the Priabonian age of the Eocene epoch (37.7-33.9 million years ago) when the area was a tropical sea[cite: 4]. It features local flora like oak and chestnut, and fauna such as roe deer and buzzards[cite: 4].
*   **Fontana dei Xotta:** A mid-19th-century fountain restored in 2004, serving as a vital watering point for local wildlife, including frogs, newts, and badgers[cite: 4].
*   **Additional Locations:** Includes Parco Arganè, Altopiano del faedo casaron, and Buso della rana (where researchers have counted 32,853 frogs)[cite: 4].

## 📈 Business Model & Financials
The financial plan leverages Premium Subscriptions, Museum Revenue-Sharing, and B2B Sponsorships[cite: 4].
*   **Fixed Costs:** €12,000[cite: 4].
*   **Break-Even Point (BEP):** Projected to be reached at Month 18[cite: 4].
*   **Revenue Projections:** Expected to scale from €15,000 in Year 1 to €88,000 in Year 2, reaching €280,000 by Year 3[cite: 4].
